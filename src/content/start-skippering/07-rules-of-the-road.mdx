Outside of racing, every vessel follows the **International Regulations for Preventing Collisions at Sea
(COLREGs)**. They decide who must keep out of the way — and that **everyone** must try to avoid a collision.

The three key rules between sailing vessels (COLREG Rule 12):

1. **Opposite tacks** — the boat on **port tack** keeps out of the way.
2. **Same tack** — the **windward** boat keeps out of the way.
3. If a port-tack boat **can’t tell** which tack a windward boat is on, it keeps out of the way.

Plus: an **overtaking** vessel always keeps clear (Rule 13) — even a sailing boat overtaking a motor boat.

## Power and sail

Generally **power gives way to sail**, but there are important exceptions:

- When the sailing boat is **overtaking**.
- A sailing boat must **not impede** a vessel that can only navigate safely within a **narrow channel** (Rule 9) — think ferries and large vessels in the river channels.
- Vessels that are **restricted** (e.g. dredging, constrained by draft) or **not under command**.
- When your engine is running and driving the boat, **you are a power vessel** — even with sails up.

## Risk of collision

If another boat’s **compass bearing doesn’t change** while it gets closer, you’re on a collision course. Take a bearing
or line it up with a stanchion and watch.

## Give-way and stand-on

- **Give-way vessel**: act **early** and make it **obvious** — a big change of course, ideally passing astern.
- **Stand-on vessel**: hold your course and speed so the other boat can predict you — but if they don’t act, you must take action to avoid a collision.

## Sound signals

| Signal | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| 1 short blast | I am altering course to starboard |
| 2 short blasts | I am altering course to port |
| 3 short blasts | I am operating astern propulsion |
| 5+ short blasts | I don’t understand your intentions / doubt — danger! |

## Navigation lights (basics)

- **Sidelights**: red on port, green on starboard.
- **Sternlight**: white, facing aft.
- **Under sail only**: sidelights + sternlight.
- **Under engine**: add a white **masthead (steaming) light** facing forward.

If you see **red and green together**, a boat is coming straight at you.
