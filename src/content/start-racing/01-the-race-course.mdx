Racing turns everything you’ve learned into a game: the same rules, the same boats, and the fastest crew round the
course wins.

## Common courses

<CourseDiagram />

Most racing marks are rounded **to port** (keep the mark on your left) unless the instructions say otherwise. A
**gate** is two marks side by side — you sail between them and round either one.

## The paperwork

- **Notice of Race (NoR)** — the event: dates, who can enter, fees.
- **Sailing Instructions (SIs)** — how *this* race works: course, marks, signals, time limits, special rules. **Read them every time.**
- **The Racing Rules of Sailing (RRS)** — the international rule book, updated every four years. The current edition is **2025–2028**.

## Before the start

1. Check in with the race committee if required (sail past the committee boat).
2. Read the **course board** on the committee boat.
3. Sail part of the first beat to check the wind — which side is favoured, how shifty it is.
4. Find the start line and check which end is favoured (next lessons).
5. Start your watch on the **warning signal**.
