Marks are where boats bunch up — and where races are often won and lost. Rule 18 sorts out who gets **room** at a
mark.

## The zone and mark-room

The **zone** is the area within **three hull lengths** of the mark. What matters is the situation when the first boat
**enters the zone**:

<MarkRounding />

- **Overlapped** when the first boat reaches the zone → the **inside** boat is entitled to **mark-room**: room to sail to the mark and round it as needed to sail the course.
- **Not overlapped** → the boat **clear ahead** gets mark-room. A boat behind that gets an inside overlap later is *not* entitled to room.

<Callout type="rule" title="Exceptions">
  Rule 18 doesn’t apply between boats on opposite tacks on a beat to windward (port/starboard still applies), and at a
  starting mark surrounded by water before the start. There are also special limits on boats that tack inside the zone
  at a windward mark. Check the current rulebook for the details.
</Callout>

## Wide in, close out

Approach a little **wide** of the mark, start the turn early, and finish the rounding **close** to the mark. You leave
heading on your new course without drifting away — and no one can slip in on the inside behind you.

## Rounding checklist

- **Before the mark:** plan the drop/hoist, sheets ready, decide which side of the next leg you want.
- **At the mark:** smooth turn, trim sails as you turn (the trimmers move with the helm).
- **After the mark:** look for clear air, tidy up the lines.

## Touching a mark

Touching a mark is a breach of **rule 31**. Take a **One-Turn Penalty** (one tack and one gybe) as soon as possible after getting clear.
