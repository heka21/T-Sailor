When boats meet while racing, the **Racing Rules of Sailing, Part 2** decide who must keep clear. They’re
similar to the COLREGs but more precise — and they apply from the preparatory signal.

## The four basic rules

<RightOfWay />

| Rule | Situation | Who keeps clear |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **10** | Opposite tacks | **Port-tack** boat |
| **11** | Same tack, **overlapped** | **Windward** boat |
| **12** | Same tack, **not overlapped** | Boat **clear astern** |
| **13** | Tacking (past head to wind, not yet close-hauled) | The **tacking** boat |

<Callout type="rule" title="Overlapped?">
  Two boats on the same tack are <strong>overlapped</strong> when neither is clear astern — i.e. part of one boat is
  ahead of a line drawn abeam from the aftermost point of the other.
</Callout>

## The limits on right of way

Having right of way doesn’t mean you can do anything:

- **Rule 14 — Avoid contact.** Every boat must avoid contact if reasonably possible. A right-of-way boat that could have avoided a collision causing damage can be penalised too.
- **Rule 15 — Acquiring right of way.** If you gain right of way by your own action (e.g. you establish an overlap to leeward, or tack in front), you must initially give the other boat **room to keep clear**.
- **Rule 16 — Changing course.** When a right-of-way boat changes course, it must give the other boat room to keep clear.
- **Rule 17 — On the same tack; proper course.** A boat that gets an overlap to leeward from clear astern (within two hull lengths) must not sail above her proper course while the overlap lasts.

## Hailing

Calls make intentions clear and avoid protests:

- “**Starboard!**” — I’m on starboard; you (on port) need to keep clear.
- “**Up, up!**” — windward boat, keep clear (leeward boat luffing).
- “**Hold your course!**” — port boat saying they’ll duck behind you.
- “**Room!**” — at a mark or obstruction (next lesson).

## Practical tips

- On port tack approaching starboard boats: **duck** (bear away behind) early and smoothly — it costs much less than a penalty.
- Tack well clear — not right under someone’s bow (rule 13 then rule 15).
- If in doubt, keep clear. Protests and penalty turns are slower than avoiding the situation.
